# Cubbl

**Stack high. Stay steady.**

Build a tower of pastel houses with a tap, then see how long you can keep it standing. Chase Perfect landings, grow your streak, and beat your best before the whole stack comes tumbling down.

## Make every drop count

Watch the crane, pick your moment, and tap to drop. As your tower climbs, the crane swings faster and uneven floors start to sway. A carefully placed house on the opposite side can help restore balance. Too far, and you might bring it all down.

You have three lives for missed drops. A collapsing tower ends the run, so make that next landing count.

## Your little city, wherever you are

Stack above a soft city skyline, from bright mornings to glowing nights. Changing skies bring a fresh mood to each run, while cheerful chimes celebrate your streaks.

Play offline on Android 11 and later. Pause when you need a break, then come back for another shot at your personal best.
